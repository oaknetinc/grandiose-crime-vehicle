Mission briefing

The Plan

12-plus years of waiting builds character. It also builds expectations. Here's our heist, in three easy steps.

  1. 01

    Hold

    Grab some $GCV, park it, and keep both hands on the wheel. The longer you ride, the more you count.

  2. 02

    GTA VI drops & we judge it

    Release day arrives. The community plays it, rates it, and decides: did it meet expectations, or not?

  3. 03

    Then one of two endings

    👍 It slaps

    Dev fees get claimed and shared with the longest-term holders, pro rata.

    👎 It flops

    We roll up our sleeves and build a better GTA VI around this project.

The holder promise

Fees & Holders

Dev fees will NOT be claimed until GTA VI launches.

  • If GTA VI meets expectations: the fees get claimed and shared among the longest-term holders.
  • Pro rata: your share depends on how much you bought and how long you held it.
  • Every holder is included. Big bag or tiny bag, early or late, you're in the crew.

How pro rata works

Amount × Time held = Your weight

Your slice of the fees = your weight ÷ everyone's weight.
